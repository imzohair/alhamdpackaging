About Alhamd Packaging

The Founder’s Note

Every product has a story. We give it a presentation that it deserves.

What made us step into the packaging industry? To be honest, it was pure fascination.

Packaging sits at the intersection of everything: heavy industrial machinery, intricate handwork, structural engineering, and creative design. Virtually every industry on earth—from local bakeries and garment exporters to electronics manufacturers—relies on packaging to survive and grow. That diversity means every single day brings a new product to learn about, a new puzzle to solve, and a new business owner to partner with.

We started Alhamd because we noticed a huge gap in the market. Too many suppliers treat boxes as cheap commodities, ignoring clean manufacturing, structural strength, and visual appeal. We saw an opportunity to do things differently: to bring creativity, surgical cleanliness, and genuine reliability back to packaging.

As a young, growing company in Kolkata, we don't just want to sell you corrugated boxes. We want to understand your product, protect your margin, and give your customers an unboxing experience they remember.

Answers Before You Buy

How do I know I won’t lose money on shipping fees?
Courier companies charge you for both the space your box takes up and its weight. Even a small gap adds extra bills. Our smart fit boxes are crafted for your products only. So, you don’t pay any extra charges.
I sell Food and Sweets. Will the boxes smell?
No. Most local suppliers store cardboard on dusty floors near machinery oil. We manufacture our food boxes in a dedicated clean space. So, you get 100% odor-free, dust-free, and safe food boxes.
What if my boxes arrive late and my packing line stops?
We are your local partner, so you don’t have to wait for long-distance trucks or unresponsive suppliers. We give you a clear delivery date and keep local stocks ready, so your business runs smoothly.
Can I stop using plastic bubble wrap without breakage?
We design smart paper inserts that hold your product firmly inside the box. Your goods won’t rattle around during transport. So, you won’t see breakage or unnecessary plastic waste.
How do I test your quality before risking a large order?
You don’t have to pay a single penny. Send us your product size and need today, and we will deliver a custom-made sample box to you within 48 hours for free.
